Privacy Policy — C2 Atom Grid Enhancer

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This policy describes how C2 Atom Grid Enhancer handles information to help users view, organize, and follow up on C2 Atom tickets. The extension is an independent personal project shared with coworkers.

Information the extension handles

  • Ticket information displayed on supported c2atom.com pages, including ticket numbers and internal identifiers, summaries or request text, status, creation dates, assignment, requester names, and unread indicators. Ticket text can contain personal communications or other information entered into C2.
  • Names and the ticket creator's email address displayed in C2, used to prepare an employee search in VAULT.
  • Information you enter into the extension, including your C2 resource name, personal notes, flags, reminders, Today selections, appearance preferences, and configuration settings.
  • Backup files you explicitly select for import.

These data are used to provide the extension's visible features. The extension does not request C2 or VAULT passwords, read authentication cookies, record general browsing history, or log keystrokes, mouse movements, or browsing activity for analytics.

Storage and synchronization

Personal ticket annotations, short ticket-summary snapshots, ticket-number mappings, and completion and reminder information are stored in your browser using Chrome's local extension storage. They are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Preferences and flag definitions, including your resource name, use Chrome's sync storage. Chrome may synchronize this information through your Google account according to your browser settings. Google's handling of synchronized data is governed by its privacy policy and Chrome settings.

The extension does not add its own encryption to locally stored annotations or exported backups.

Sharing and external services

When you activate Open in VAULT, the extension opens an HTTPS employee-search page at vault.lbpsb.qc.ca. The search URL includes the selected employee name or email address. VAULT receives that query and handles the request under its own access controls and data practices. Your browser may retain the search URL in its history, and the receiving service may log requests. The extension does not send your personal ticket notes to VAULT.

Export creates a JSON file containing your annotations, saved ticket summaries, and flag definitions. Import reads a file you select. Exported files remain wherever you save or share them. Selecting a saved ticket in the popup copies its ticket number to your clipboard.

The extension has no advertising, analytics, or telemetry service. It does not sell user data or use it for unrelated purposes, creditworthiness, or lending. User data is used or transferred only as needed for the described user-facing features. This use follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your choices

You can edit or clear individual ticket annotations and use Backup & data > Clear all to delete all saved annotations, including completed notes. This control does not reset preferences, flag definitions, or the separate ticket-number mapping index. Preferences and definitions can be edited in the popup and advanced options.

When the extension observes a completed ticket with no unread message, it clears its personal flags and reminders. Written notes and a ticket snapshot are retained in Completed notes until removed. Other saved annotations have no fixed automatic expiry.

Chrome manages extension storage and removes local extension data when the extension is uninstalled. Chrome synchronization and any browser-level backups are governed by your Chrome settings. Exported files and clipboard contents are separate and must be managed independently.

Disabling the extension stops its features; it does not delete stored annotations. The extension does not change server-side C2 ticket records. Removing extension data does not delete information already held by C2 or VAULT.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email published on the C2 Atom Grid Enhancer Chrome Web Store listing. If you contact the developer, the information you choose to send will be used to respond to your request.

This policy will be updated when the extension's data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
